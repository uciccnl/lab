description: "Hao Liu received his B.E. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University, where he worked in Professor Hang Su and Jun Zhu’s group on multi-agent reinforcement learning, and spent a summer at MIT B&C with Josh Tenenbaum and Tianmin Shu working on machine Theory of Mind. He then completed his M.A. in Psychology at New York University, where he worked with Sebastian Michelmann and Marcelo Mattar on using diffusion models as a generative account of human episodic memory. At UCI he is co-advised by Aaron Bornstein and Mark Steyvers. His research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and computational modeling of cognition — reinforcement learning, multi-agent systems, memory, and theory of mind — with continuing interests in philosophy of mind and mathematics. Outside the lab, he enjoys LEGO, photography, video games, hiking, and reading."
