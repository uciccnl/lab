description: "
Alexa obtained her B.S. in cognitive neuroscience at UC Davis. After graduating she worked with Dr. Timothy Hanks studying perceptual decision-making. Following this she worked with Dr. Manish Saggar at Stanford researching brain dynamics through topological data analysis. As a PhD student Alexa is co-advised by Dr. Liz Chrastil, and studies how we represent experiences and use these memory representations to make decisions about the world. She investigates how people use mental maps or “cognitive graphs” to organize complex relationships as associative networks. Her research explores the intersection of environmental structure, active exploration, and individual memory differences. Outside the lab she enjoys reading, art, and being outdoors.
